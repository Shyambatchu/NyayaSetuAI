## Prepared Q&A

| Question | Honest answer outline |
|---|---|
| Is this the government's Nyaya Setu? | No. VidhiVerse AI is independently developed and not affiliated with the Government of India. The positioning is complementary, and we link to government services. |
| How does it differ from Nyaya Setu? | We make no claims about gaps. Our focus is document explanations, evidence-linked findings, legal research and guided assistance. |
| Is this still PS-02? | Yes. The conversational chatbot is the primary solution, and other capabilities support it. State the organisers' answer if D-18 has closed. |
| What if an answer is wrong? | Validation reduces fabricated citations, but can't guarantee interpretation. Sources are visible, feedback becomes regression tests, and high-impact answers get legal review. |
| What if a finding is wrong? | Evidence proves where it came from, not that the reading is right. People should confirm important points with a professional. |
| Can the AI invent a procedure? | No. It can only match reviewed workflows and explain them. |
| Could someone miss a deadline? | Reminders only reflect dates users confirm. We make no guarantee, and tell them to check with the authority. |
| Do you replace lawyers? | No. It is general information and preparation. |
| Is the VidhiVerse AI name protected? | Not yet known. Trademark, domain and brand-availability checks are open, and we make no such claim. |
| Is it DPDP compliant? | Designed for minimal data and user control. No compliance claim; assessment needed. |
| Coverage? | State the actual count of verified Acts, judgments and workflows. |
| Business model? | Free core; later subscriptions and institutional plans; grants. Unvalidated. |
